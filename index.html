DWS Gmail

DWS Gmail is a personal productivity tool. Its owner is its only user. It is not offered to the public.

Privacy policy

What it accesses. With the owner's permission, and only for the owner's own account, DWS Gmail reads Gmail messages, creates Gmail drafts, adds Google Calendar events, and reads and writes Google Drive files.

How the data is used. The tool uses this data only to help the owner keep track of his own work. It never sends email to anyone other than the owner. Content may be processed by the AI assistant the owner uses, Anthropic's Claude, to help organize his tasks.

Where the data is kept. The tool stores what it keeps on the owner's own computer. The data is not sold, shared, or transferred to anyone else.
